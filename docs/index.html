Inventory and sites on the Bangladesh map

Points are the 411 inventory records; boxes are the 83 site windows, outlined by their low-flow result. Rivers are HydroRIVERS reaches with mean flow of 150 m³/s or more, drawn wider for larger rivers; borders are Natural Earth. The map opens on the northern border belt: scroll or pinch to zoom, drag to pan, click a box to open that site below.

Where extraction had begun, year by year

Drag the slider or press Play. A site lights up in its result colour from the first January in which the low-flow series shows an extraction-type change (pits, floodplain pits, possible dredging or bar loss); sites that changed within the two seasons before the chosen year carry a dark ring. Squares are depots, stockpile yards and sand-filled land. Hollow markers are dated sites that have not yet changed; faint dots are sites with no dated extraction signal. Bar-vegetation sites (char cropping) are not extraction and stay faint. Click a lit site to open its series below.

When change shows up, by position along the border

Analysed sites from west (top) to east (bottom), coloured by result. Bars span the visual onset read from the January panels; dots mark the statistical break in low-flow water area (Pettitt test, filled when p < 0.05). Dashed lines are dated laws, bans and dredging works, numbered as in the list below the chart.

    Which way the extraction spread

    First year of change at each site with an extraction signal, plotted against its position along the border (west to east). Larger dots are pit extraction (in the channel or as floodplain pit fields). Dashed lines mark the 2010 Sand and Soil Management Act and the 2014 ban on mechanical quarrying in Sylhet.
